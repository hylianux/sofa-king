# Recorded instruments

The notes in these folders come from the **Fluid R3 GM** sound font by Frank Wen, as rendered by
[MIDI.js Soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) (Benjamin Gleitzman).
They're used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/).

Changes: two notes per octave (C and F sharp, C2 to C8) were kept, each cut to 2.5 seconds with a
half-second fade at the end, and turned into mono Opus files to keep them small.
