# YouTube and VacuumTube

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

## Where YouTube opens

**Settings → YouTube → Where it opens** decides where everything YouTube goes: the YouTube tile, *Find it on YouTube*, trailers, and YouTube Music on album pages.

- **Automatic** (the default): [VacuumTube](https://github.com/shy1132/VacuumTube) if it's installed, otherwise YouTube for TV.
- **YouTube for TV, here:** YouTube's own TV page (the one smart TVs show), inside the app. The D-pad, A and B work as on a TV.
- **The website:** youtube.com, as it looks on a computer.

VacuumTube is the nicer way to watch YouTube on a TV, if you're happy to install it. It opens with your own profile, and searches and albums open straight in it. If it's installed somewhere the app can't find, tell it where in **Settings → Apps**.

## Extras for YouTube for TV

All of these are off until you switch them on (VacuumTube has its own):

- **Block ads:** no ad breaks, ad tiles or sponsored results.
- **SponsorBlock:** skips sponsor spots, and anything else you pick.
- **Return YouTube Dislike:** shows likes and dislikes when a video starts.
- **DeArrow:** plainer titles in place of clickbait ones.

## YouTube Music

YouTube Music's button on an album's page plays the whole album, in VacuumTube or YouTube for TV. On an album you don't have, picking a song plays just that song.
