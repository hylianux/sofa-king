# Themes, layouts and sounds

[← Back to the README](../README.md)

**Settings → Look** sets how the app looks and sounds. A highlighted choice shows in a small preview on the right before you pick it.

## Layouts

How Home is arranged, and how the controller moves around it. Any layout works with any mood.

- **Big view** (the default): Netflix-style. The selected title fills the top of the screen, with one row at a time below.
- **Rows:** several rows of posters at once.
- **Big posters:** one row at a time, with large posters.
- **Grid:** wide picture tiles, like Roku or Apple TV.
- **PlayStation 5:** one row of square tiles, with the selected title's picture behind.
- **PS3 cross bar:** sections across the top, titles down a list.

## Changing the pieces

**Advanced theming** changes any one piece:

- **Colours:** 22 schemes, including Dracula, Nord, Gruvbox, Catppuccin, Light and High contrast, plus the highlight colour.
- **Fonts:** 15, for headings and text, including the easy-to-read Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- **Sounds:** 14 sound packs, and an **instrument** to play them on (chimes, 8-bit, synths, or recorded piano, harp, guitar and more). **Sound effects** like echo and bitcrush add on top.
- **Background:** a moving backdrop: PS3 wave, blurry lights, aurora, flowing colours, starfield or particles.
- **Effects:** as many as you like: snow, rain, film grain, scanlines, sparkles, mist, embers, fireflies, falling leaves, hearts, bubbles, confetti and more.

Everything that moves holds still with **Settings → General → Screen → Animations** off.

## Saving your own

**Saved themes** has ten squares. Choose an empty one to save the look you have now; choose a full one to use it.

You can also **make your own** moods, layouts, colours, fonts, sound packs, instruments, effects and backgrounds. They're plain files in `~/.config/sofa-king/themes/`, which the app sets up with templates. [The theme guide](../themes/README.md) explains how. Themes from other people are checked before they're used: nothing can load from the internet or run code.
